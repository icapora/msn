# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and assuming good faith
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private security advisory or a
direct message. Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate.
